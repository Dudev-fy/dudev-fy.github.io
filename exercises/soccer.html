Learn about three of the world's most famous footballers

10

Lionel Messi

Lionel Messi is an Argentine footballer known for his incredible dribbling, passing, vision and goal scoring. He spent many years at Barcelona before playing for Paris Saint-Germain and later Inter Miami.

Messi has won the FIFA World Cup with Argentina and is widely regarded as one of the greatest footballers in history.

10

Neymar Jr.

Neymar is a Brazilian footballer famous for his skill, creativity, speed and flair. He became a star at Santos before moving to Barcelona and later Paris Saint-Germain.

Neymar has represented Brazil at major international tournaments and is one of the country's most famous modern footballers.

7

Cristiano Ronaldo

Cristiano Ronaldo is a Portuguese footballer known for his finishing, athleticism, heading ability and goal scoring. He has played for clubs including Sporting CP, Manchester United, Real Madrid, Juventus and Al-Nassr.

Ronaldo has won major domestic and European trophies and has represented Portugal for many years.
